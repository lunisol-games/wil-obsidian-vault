Each NPC in the brewery will have different actions available to them.

Some actions are Instant, other have a Duration.
Some actions are done Solo, other require the [[Player]] to accompany the NPC. Some may also involve other NPCs (e.g. a threesome)
Some actions have costs such as Gold, Magima.
Some actions have unlock conditions such as story progress.
Some actions have execution condition such as time of day, horniness level...
Some actions are one time thing (e.g. researching a new recipe), others may evolve on consecutive use, and some may just draw from a random pool.
