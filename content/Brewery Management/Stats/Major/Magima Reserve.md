Each Witch has a reserve of Magima caracterized by a current value and a cap.
When a Witch is inactive, she regenerates some.

Activities that require use of magic will have a Magima Reserve requirement. Player has the option to do a Transfer, which quickly regenerates some, in case she has not enough.

As the player progresses, they may improve the effectiveness of Transfer, and unlock "new ways" of doing Transfers... if you catch my drift ;) !
As the Witch's [[Affinity Level]] augments, the cap and base regeneration may improve too.
