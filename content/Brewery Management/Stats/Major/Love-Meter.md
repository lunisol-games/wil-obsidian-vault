[[Helios's Temple in the Brewery]] gets fueled by love. Basically any action from a bashful kiss to a sex scene generate points that allow to go to the next threshold and progress the story.

It's a [[Leveling System]] presented in a bit of a Meta way, like an interface created by Helios so your human mind can "comprehend" what you need to do and track your progression!

Actions with Witches bring more points. Replaying an event gives way less point than the first time to encourage finding new content instead of farming the same scene again.
