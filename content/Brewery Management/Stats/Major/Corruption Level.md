It's a [[Leveling System]] that lives on the Player. Not in the Demo. It measures how much you've fallen to your desires and let [[Helios]] corrupt your [[Concepts/Magima]]. It appears once you reach the first level with the [[Love-Meter]].

Higher Corruption level means :
- Changes in the Dialogues with the Witches.
- New CGs or variations of existing CGs. If we commit to new CGs, they should be buyable to Maja as sort of "What Ifs" scenarios if we're going True Love route.
- Potentially access to certain upgrades that make the game easier. As a sort of "temptation" like the parasite tadpoles in BG3.
