A progress bar used by different systems from the game.

- It has a Current Level.
- It has a Current XP and XP cap.
- When Current XP reaches XP cap, it can't go any higher.
- If XP is maxed, and, eventually, another condition is met (e.g. just wait for tomorrow for an event to happen in the morning), the Current Level is incremented, and Current XP goes to 0, XP cap adjusts to the new level's table.
- The level of a system can impact different things. For exemple:
	- Content may be unlocked by that.
	- Previous content can also evolve.
	- Effectiveness of the brewery (potions' price, costs of things) may evolve.
- Some of these systems can also lose xp, after a challenge failure for exemple.
	- However it can't go below 0 and you can't lose a level to avoid game-breaking stuff.
