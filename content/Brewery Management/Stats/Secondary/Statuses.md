These are effects that can be tied to all sort of things that will impact the game in various ways. They are visible to the player and usually encourage them to engage with the game in a certain way to take advantage of it. Some of the effects are explicitely described, others may be hidden and implicetely guessed.

Most Statuses are **Temporary**. They elapse after a certain number of **days**.
Other are **Event-Driven**. They elapse after a certain thing happens.
Others are **Infinite**. They never disappear.

A few exemples of Statuses.

**RAINY DAY (global Status) (1 day left)**: It's raining today. The forest will Yield more ingredients. The Volcano will yield less ingredients. *(Hidden: a certain activity will trigger an event related to rain and unlock a new CG).*

**HELIOS WOMB TATOO (Anna Status)**: Having sex replenishes Magima entirely. (Hidden: random daily events can occur. Can't be removed)

**HIGH MOTIVATION (Belle Status) (2 days left)**: Brewing costs 1 less Time and 10 less Magima.

**POWERS CHARGED (Player Status)**: Helios' power flow strongly in your veins. *(Hidden: enables certain dialogue options and event to happen. Disappears once it's used. Reappears 3 days after.)*
