## Work

> [!NOTE] Sell at Shroomburg Market (Solo)
> Sell up to 2 potions of your choice. Costs 50 Magima, 3 Times.

> [!NOTE] Marketing Campaign : Healing Potion (Duo)
> Gain a small amount of reputation, and improves Healing Potion selling price. Costs 50 Magima, a Healing Potion and 3 Times. Repeatable X scene at the end to generate Helios Influence and gain XP.

## Transfer

> [!NOTE] Direct Transfer (Duo)
> Standard Transfer method. Costs 2 Times. Grants 50 Magima.

## Bond

> [!NOTE] Ask for a dream
> Unlocks a new dream that will be triggered on next night. Option appears only when there is a new unlockable dream. Gold cost grows each time it's used.
