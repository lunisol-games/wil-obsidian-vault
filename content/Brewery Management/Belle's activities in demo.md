## Work

> [!NOTE] Brew a Healing Potion (Solo)
> Costs 25 Magima, 3 Times, 2 Honey, 2 Redcaps.

> [!NOTE] Brew a Stamina Potion (Solo)
> Costs 35 Magima, 1 Naught Root, 3 Honey. Requires Research to be finished.

> [!NOTE] Research on Naught Roots (Duo)
> Progress research on Stamina Potion (3 steps). Costs 50 Magima, 1 Rare Root, and 4 Times. Repeatable X scene at the end to generate Helios Influence and gain XP.

## Transfer


> [!NOTE] Hand Transfer (Duo)
> Least effective Transfer method. Costs 4 Times. Grants 50 Magima

> [!NOTE] Direct Transfer (Duo)
> Standard Transfer method. Costs 2 Times. Grants 50 Magima

## Bond

> [!NOTE] Convince to use Direct Transfer (Challenge) 
> Unlocks "Direct Transfer". Costs 2 Times. One try per day.
