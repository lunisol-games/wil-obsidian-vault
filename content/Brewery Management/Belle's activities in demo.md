## Work

> [!NOTE] Brew a Healing Potion (Solo)
> Costs 25 Magima, 3 Times

> [!NOTE] Brew a Lightness Potion (Solo)
> Costs 30 Magima, 3 Times. Need Study : Naught Root succeeded.

> [!NOTE] Research on Magima (Duo)
> 2 Times and an ingredient that hasn't been used in this activity yet. Stems from Belle wanting to understand the reason why the magic is so strange and corrupted around Shroomburg. Unlocked after convinced to use Direct Transfer as a "okay but you gotta help me figure it out". 
> This improve the "Research level" of Belle which unlocks something new at each Threshold.
> The first unlock is after 3 researches : Improve permanently the Transfer action to grant 10 more Magima.
> For some ingredients, more rare, like the Naught Root, Belle will discover a strange Magic property and this will unlock a Study challenge for that.

> [!NOTE] Study : Naught Root (Challenge)
Costs 50 Magima, 2 Times. Repeatable X scene at the end to generate Helios Influence and gain XP. Unlocked after researching Naught Root. This event series introduces first hints on Helios influence in the area and how it corrupts the magic with naughty tricks. Player and Belle get magnetized into naughty poses during the research and in the end Belle understand its inner workings and reverse engineers a process to create the Potion of Lightness, that temporarily reduces the user weight, or more precisely the gravity they're under. Useful for Anna in her expeditions to traverse rough terrain.
