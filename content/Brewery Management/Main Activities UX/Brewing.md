The Brewing system is fairly similar to Potionomics.
You pick a potion recipe, and then you select ingredients that contribute to the "elements" of that recipe.

![[PotionomicsBrew.png]]
*Main screen reference from Potionomics*

- Ingredients are placed in the Cauldron. Each ingredient costs 10 Magima to use by default.
- The sum of element type in the cauldron is calculated and we check if the balance is close enough to the recipe ratio.
- The sum then determines the power threshold of the potion (10 levels).
- If it's at least lvl1, and the balance is okay, it can be brewed.
