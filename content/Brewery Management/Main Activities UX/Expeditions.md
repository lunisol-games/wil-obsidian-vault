Everyday, Anna can venture to one "Zone" near Shroomburg. The Woods, the Lake, the Mountain...
Each zone has its quirks. Monsters, obstacles, quest items to find...
Each zone also has an ingredient loot table. When an ingredient is looted it's rolled from this table.

When preparing for an expedition, player selects an area to preview its path. It shows a succession of things Anna will go through. There are 3 type of things:
- Monsters. They reduce Anna's Energy.
- Hazards. They do all sort of things.
- Reward. Either an ingredient, a quest item, or even an event (rarer)
	- If an event occurs while Anna is out, it will be shown from her PoV when we go back to the Expedition Room before we get the reward. 
	- To make things more believable, events would be at the end of the route, as something like the target to reach to progress the game. That way it smoothly transitions to her teleporting back.

The game will use a fairly similar system to the minigame in Potionomics, although i'd simplify it to only use "Energy" as she is not a fighter and it's more a matter of going around danger than killing monsters.
