A clan composed exclusively of Witches, that live in [[The Shades]].

They are forbidden from commiting with a man and having children, so all the Witches here are adopted from various places of the world.

They work together in heightening their powers, researching magic, and plot to ensure the Witches keep their powerful hold over the different regions. Although they are starting to have some serious problems with [[New Dawn]] and a war may be brewing.

They also guard [[The Moontree]] and make sure no one ever gets close to it.
