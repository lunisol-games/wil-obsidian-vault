Both of them were activists in their youth, opposing the anti-witch policies that were brewing in Rospiel.

That's how they met and became close, yet their motivations were rather different.

Melissandre had a unhealthy obsessions for Heliotrope and Witches, whereas Richard was feeling oppressed because of his mark and seeked to surround himself with allies.

This is also how Richard came to acquire the Brewery. As things started to become out of control in Rospiel, he was tipped by a person of the movement about this establishment. That person knew about the Temple and wanted to make sure an ally to the cause would inherit it.
