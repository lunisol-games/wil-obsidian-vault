A group that has studied the history of Gods and Mankind, and works actively in both preserving it, and preventing a new Eclipse to take place and kill millions again. They are exclusively comprised of [[Markless]] people.

They monitor and preserve the temples of old, and keep a close eye on anyone that may interact with them... especially Heliotropes, as they are aware that Helios can converse directly with them, although they unfortunately can't listen in. However, their motto is to not interfere directly with the gods plans, unless a major crisis that may trigger an Eclipse forces their hand. That's why [[Nelson Hiltberg]] will remain a passive observer and just try to slightly nudge the player into not spreading Helios influence, but also try to get intel out of you about Helios' plan.
