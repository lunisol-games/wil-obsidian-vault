A political group with its roots in [[Rospiel]] that wants to rid [[The Northern Kingdom]], and, gradually, [[The Astra Continent]], of the presence of Witches.

They argue that their powers have for far too long broken the balance in the continent, giving honest, hard-working [[Markless]] people an unfair competition.

At the time of the story, the group has a strong hold over the Capital's policies and Witches have been outlawed from Rospiel very recently. Restlessness is growing all over the continent as the movement's influence grows more and more each day and laws become more radical.
