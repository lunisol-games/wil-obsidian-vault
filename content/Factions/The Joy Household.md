The Joy Sisters come from [[Aerafal]], a little county midway between Rospiel and Shroomburg.

Grew up in a non-witch household with loving parents that tried to accomodate their witchhood and nurtured their powers.

As fascism rises in the north, they set off to the south to be safe. Doing itinerant work here and there, they stumble upon Melissandre's job offer and give it a shot.
