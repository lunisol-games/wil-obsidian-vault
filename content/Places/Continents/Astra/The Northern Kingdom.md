One of the strongest countries on Earth, expanding on the entire top half of [[The Astra Continent]]. It's a monarchy inspired from Great Britain, with a similar political system albeit the king is NOT symbolic and exert actual executive power and has the last word on everything. The different cities are governed by a mix of elected representatives and king appointed officials that act in his stead. Although the elected ones may voice over the need of the people to the king, they have to be careful never directly oppose their sovereign or their head would roll so there still is a bit of an authoritarian vibe there.
Recently, the king has really taken a liking to the [[New Dawn]] movement, and gave it its full support, as historically Witches have always been dangerous to men in power and getting rid of them would ensure a long and safe reign.
