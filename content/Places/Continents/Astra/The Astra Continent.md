Biggest piece of land on Earth, has an overall european vibe. Mostly vertical shape with very different climate between north and south.

[[The Northern Kingdom]] is a force to reckon with there, boasting a very powerful army, advanced magic tech, and [[Rospiel]] its beautiful, beaming, capital, and most populated city in the world.

Further South is a region called [[The Emerald Vale]], a democratic republic where [[Aerafal]] and [[Shroomburg]] can be found.

There is no major conflict happening in the continent at the time of the story although fascist ideas from [[New Dawn]] are spreading southbound slowly but surely...
