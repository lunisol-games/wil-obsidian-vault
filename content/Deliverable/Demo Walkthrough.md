
# FIRST DAY - Purely story
The game starts with the event series that introduce the 5 current characters and the Brewery.
Melissandre is briefly introduced with Nelson.
Before meeting the Witches, the player can ask a few questions to Melissandre about how things stand currently, what's his/her role is in the brewery, etc...
Each Witch encounter start with a few questions the player can ask about them and their powers, followed by tutorialization of their job (Expedition, Brew, Sales). Then, a naughty event occurs for each of them.
The Temple is vaguely introduced but its secrets are not revealed yet.
The player goes to sleep, a bit shaken up by the quite hectic day they had, and the Challenge system is tutorialized as they struggle to fall asleep.

# THE REOPENING - Start of the "open world"
At this point, the player is free to interact with the 4 girls in the order they want. The only forced event is the (re)inauguration ceremony that takes place in the evening of the second day of game time.

Each Witch has a first little questline related to their job. Anna has to go past the Naughtree in the Deerwoods and will require your help. Belle needs to figure out what's going on with Magima in Shroomburg before she can start to trust you... but you can't talk about the Temple to her, right? Maja does some networking in town and requires your help to market the Stamina Potion and start to have people talking about the brewery. 
All these questlines require to interact with the game to some degree : find certain ingredients, gather certain amount of money, and are here to further familiarize the player with the main gameplay loop.

As for Melissandre, she may answer a few of your question, but she encourages you to deepen your bonds with your Witches before she can reveal something important to you.

# THE RITUAL
When all mini-questlines have been cleared, Melissandre says that you're ready. She invites you to have sex under Helios' statue. She explains to you her ambitions of having you build your Witch harem in the brewery as you fuck. When the money time comes, the player is transported to Helios' realm and have a talk directly with the god.
Helios explains, in a rather boorish fashion, that he's going the player to get all the ladies, as long as they follow his advices. He explains he wants the player to kiss and hug and fuck a lot of different girls to charge up the temple's [[Love-Meter]]. He says that in exchange, he'll give him gifts that any mortal man would sell their soul for. He concludes that if all goes well, he'll get to fuck Selene herself. The player is sent back and has the most philosophical post nut clarity ever, on which we could close the demo.
