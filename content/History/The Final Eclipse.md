The name of the plan Helios came up with to unite with Selene forever.

It consists of invading the Moontree, heavily guarded by the Tyranne Clan, meet with Selene, and distract her long enough for Helios to bind himself to the Moontree.

Depending on how Helios power have been exploited by Polymede, different endings will occur.

The "bad" ending will trigger a soft extinction of humans, forcefully bringing them all to Helios' domain to partake in an eternal soul orgy. And, of course, Selene would be his and only his, and be the queen of his kingdom, his main concubine.

It's bittersweet because Selene, as much as she adores Helios, also loves the humans and it's unfair that Helios decided that for her. Plus this new relationship is not super equal.

The True ending involves having all the cards in hand to deviate helios plan into something less extreme that would still satisfy him. The Faalklands become a haven for Heliotropes and Witches alike, and they get prepared and taught the truth about the gods and their history, as well as their fate in the afterlife. Eclipses can happen regularly without hurting the life on earth because of a magic tech upkept by the Tyranne clan.
