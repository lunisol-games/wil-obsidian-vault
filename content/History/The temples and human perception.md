A long way before the first Witch and Heliotrope appeared, Selene and Helios were worshipped and loved by humans. They built statues and temples to their likeness.

The faith and adoration amplified the gods influence there, bringing forth boons and blessings. Love, fertility, good fortune, good crops...

When the second eclypse and its terrifying tremors happened, their perception of the gods changed. They were misled to think Helios and Selene were mortal enemies and their unending war brought calamities such as the Eclipse.

In the era of Witches, when it was understood that Heliotropes were depriving Witches from the Magima supplies, Helios started to be villainized by Witches and their supporters. This is why most temples to Helios were destroyed during that era, whereas sanctuaries were created to protect the ones from Selene.
