At the dawn of the universe, Selene dwells within the Moon Tree on a vast, lush, wild, and dangerous Earth, one that is still devoid of human life. 
Orbiting around this planet is Helios and his bright Sun, a small yet intense star that brings light to this otherwise dark and cold world.

Helios gazes in fascination at Selene’s creations. She endlessly weaves new creatures and plants into existence with her beautiful magic, drawing upon the inexhaustible Magima of the Moon Tree. Curious to catch a glimpse of the beautiful Selene, he tries to approach her so he can admire her more closely. But although the goddess is just as eager to meet her soulmate, she is terribly shy and constantly turns away to hide from him, thus creating the rotation of the Earth. As a result, the small, crescent-shaped island on which the Moon Tree stands remains perpetually shrouded in darkness.

By a fortunate miracle, after millions of years, Helios finally manages to position himself directly above the Moon Tree, bathing it in his light for the first time in the history of the two celestial bodies. The two gods’ eyes meet, and they fall madly in love with each other. Selene is invited aboard Helios’s star, and the two unite for the very first time. They wish they could remain together like this for all eternity, but the phenomenon that would later be known as the Eclipse plunges the rest of the planet into total darkness, as the Moon Tree absorbs all of Helios’s light.  When Selene realized it, she told Helios they had to separate and hid once more to protect her creations from total annihilation.

Their union, however, will bring about a miracle. The seed Helios planted within Selene germinates a few years later, giving rise to the first humans: mortal beings created in their image.
