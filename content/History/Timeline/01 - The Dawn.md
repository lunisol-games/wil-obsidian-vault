At the dawn of the universe, Selene dwells within [[The Moontree]] on a vast, lush, wild, and dangerous Earth, one that is still devoid of human life. 
Orbiting around this planet is Helios and his bright Sun, a small yet intense star that brings light to this otherwise dark and cold world.

Helios gazes in fascination at Selene’s creations. She endlessly weaves new creatures and plants into existence with her beautiful magic, drawing upon the inexhaustible Magima of the Tree. He tried to help as much as he could, bathing them in his light, so vital for life.

Curious to catch a glimpse of the beautiful Selene, he tries to approach her so he can admire her more closely. But although the goddess is just as eager to meet her eternal soulmate, she is terribly shy and constantly turns away to hide from him, thus creating the rotation of the Earth. As a result, [[The Dark Isles]] on which the Moon Tree stands remains perpetually shrouded in darkness.
