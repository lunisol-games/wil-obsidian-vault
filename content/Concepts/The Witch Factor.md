Witches and their descendants (even non-witches ones) inherit from darker aspects of Selene's personality. Greed, jealousy, and of course lust. While for most Witches it's pretty light, some are very "quirky" because of it.

When its influence is beyond reasonable, it's referred to as "Witch Factor". Think of it as the orange cat gene, or the Targaryen mad trait of sort. It manifests with supernatural phenomenons that are not directly linked with the witch base power. Visions. Trances and memory loss. Split personality... a Witchzophrenia of sorts.

It's pretty rare and usually makes the witch more powerful if she follows her desires. [[Tyranne, the first Witch]], was famously known for being a bit of a looney. Non-witches like [[Melissandre Redpom]] can also have cases of The Witch Factor if they descend from a Witch that had it.

Witch Factor is not an official term and the phenomenon is barely known by some experts on history like [[The Order of Sunlight]] or [[The Tyranne Sisterhood]]. It's not something that can easily be diagnosed either as it's a purely psychological affliction.
