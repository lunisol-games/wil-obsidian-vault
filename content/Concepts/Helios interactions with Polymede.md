After Melissandre unlock the temple, Polymede can converse with Helios by touching the statue in the temple. He'll remind you of the goals for the main quest and give you tips and side quests.

Helios will steer Polymede to make him his champion so that the final eclipse can happen.

The incentive he gives him: "If you follow my instructions to the letter, you'll get all the women you want, even Selene herself"

Although it's a bit twisted, it's not really a lie as Helios nature is not deceitful. But hes so boorish that he doesn't really care for the details and dont explain everything.
