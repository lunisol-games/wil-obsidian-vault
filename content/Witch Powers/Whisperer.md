Specializing in manipulating the mind and heart of others, using their alluring voice. Also capable of reading thoughts / emotions. 

This is a very rare and powerful type of witch that is universally feared.
Their powers have some limit. They can only coerce people for a very short time span and when the spell fades, the victim immediatly understands they've been bewitched.

However, if used masterfully and repeatedly, it's possible to leave long-lasting effects on someone. A few kings were, and maybe still are, puppets to the whims of a [[Whisperer]].
