The powers of witches are actually not as specific as "Brewing potion" or "Teleporting". It's more a result of how the witch talents were nurtured and what's her predisposition / passion for certain topics or activities. Witches of old, especially, didn't have named roles such as today. But because of their legacy, with books on how they trained and developped their powers, names roles have started to emerge alongside "guides" for young witches to follow.

Childhood is critical for a witch's development as learning completely new skills later on is very very difficult.

Ultimately, powers can be boiled down to three categories.

- The Body : The ability to achieve physical prowess. Strength boost, teleportation, invisibility, disguise... Usually develops on Witches that like the outdoors or that are hyperactive. [[Wayfarers]] for exemple are particularly good at this.
- The Soul : The ability to interact with other living being's essence. Mind reading, mind control, divination, empathy... Usually develops on Witches that are social and charismatic. [[Whisperer]] are experts at this and are particularly feared even among their peers.
- The World : The ability to interact with the physical world, especially [[Concepts/Magima]]. Casting elemental spells, manipulating weather, brewing potions... Usually develops better for children that are curious about how things work. [[Apothecary]] are a very good at this.

Note that, although one individual is unlikely to master these 3 categories, it's not uncommon to specialize in 2 or have at least a secondary one. [[Wayfarers]] are for exemple also not too bad at Soul Magic, and can hear the thoughts and counsels of animals and plants.
