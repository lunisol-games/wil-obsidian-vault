At the dawn of the universe, [[Selene]] dwells within [[The Moontree]] on a vast, lush, wild, and dangerous Earth, one that is still devoid of human life. 
Far away from the planet is [[Helios]] and his bright Sun, a small yet intense star that brings light to this otherwise dark and cold world.

Helios gazes in fascination at Selene’s creations. She endlessly weaves new creatures and plants into existence with her beautiful magic, drawing upon the inexhaustible Magima of the Tree. He was trying to help as much as he could, bathing them in his light, so vital for life.

He is also very curious to catch a glimpse of the beautiful Selene. But although the goddess is just as eager to meet her eternal soulmate, she is terribly shy, and makes the earth rotate in a way that would avoid direct alignment between her and him. As a result, [[The Dark Isles]] on which the Moon Tree stands remains perpetually shrouded in darkness, while the other regions all get their fair share of lighting.
