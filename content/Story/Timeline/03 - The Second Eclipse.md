For millenias, [[Selene]] averted her gaze from [[Helios]]'s star, and watched as human society grew into many wonderful countries and cultures. 

A long way before the first Witch and Heliotrope appeared, Selene and Helios were worshipped and loved by humans. They built statues and temples to their likeness.
The faith and adoration amplified the gods influence there, bringing forth boons and blessings. Love, fertility, good fortune, good crops...

But despite these cults and adoration, both of the gods were still heartbroken from their separation, after what they experienced together during [[02 - The First eclipse]]. Actually a bit more than that. They had a void to fill now, a craving. Not just romantic. Primal, sexual. They spent their days getting off thinking about each other, but it was barely enough. Witnessing all the shameful techniques humans invented in bed and not being able to try them was torture. Selene really wanted to try this "Pegging" thing with Helios :( ! Impossibly horny, about to turn crazy, Selene finally gave in and accepted the god of love's embrace a second time, bringing forth the Second Eclipse, the first one witnessed by men.

The world was shaken for a few hours. Earthquakes. Tsunamis. Volcanos. All in complete darkness. Many perished. When the second eclipse and its terrifying tremors happened, the humans perception of the gods forever changed. They were misled to think Helios and Selene were mortal enemies and their unending war brought calamities such as the Eclipse.

The post nuptial clarity was awful for Selene, who could not bear seeing her creations destroyed by her own lust. If their union was bringing such calamity everytime, would it be reasonable to ever meet again? But Selene knew that Helios, who was less empathetic towards human life, would keep chasing after her to do it again. But her main worry was herself. She felt like her self control and patience was gradually slipping. She had to come up with a plan to prevent another eclipse from happening...
