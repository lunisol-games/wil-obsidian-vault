Grew up in [[The Joy Household]] in [[Aerafal]].

Naive and gentle girl, a bit clumsy and less capable than her sister for everyday life stuff, she is nonetheless very talented in her area of expertise and has always been passionate about Alchemy. A bit too much.

She is a very curious and obsessive girl. She always had to know everything. Learn everything. Unbeknowt to anyone, not even her, she has a case of the [[The Witch Factor]], making her thirst for knowledge and power a tad too intense and uncontrollable. She hurt herself a fair amount of time because of the insane risks she was taking during her experiments, due to her recklessness.

She is in particular completely obsessed with [[Divine Drops]], a lost elixir created by [[Tyranne, the first Witch]] during her reign, to heighten her powers to the level of Selene for the span of a few seconds. It's going to be her main agenda throughout the game and the success of it is absolutely needed to get the true ending of [[The Final Eclipse]].

The themes explored throughout bonding with Belle are [[The Witch Factor]], the inner workings of [[Magima]] and potion brewing, her childhood in [[Aerafal]], and her grand quest to recreate the [[Divine Drops]].

Her character development varies depending on [[Romance Routes]].
[[Romance Routes - Love]]: Her Witch Factor mellows out and she achieves her life goal thanks to your precious help. She may use the [[Divine Drops]] to contribute to the magic tech that is needed for the True Love Ending.
[[Romance Routes - Corruption]]: Her Witch Factor is reinforced and twisted into obsession for naughty brews (although not going this route doesn't bar from brewing them!) She may create an alt version of the [[Divine Drops]] that is the Heliotrope equivalent, possibly granting you the power to transcend your humanity.
