Maja was born in the slums of Sarkas, a powerful mercantile city located in the far east of the visitable world. Whenever her past is brought up she'd depict a harsh life there but it's all a lie.

Soon after birth she was sold to a witch of the Faalklands, the island covered by the shades of the Moontree, where Tyranne led witches to build their own little kingdom. The little Witch was taken care of by the clan and followed their teachings like many other adoptees from around the world.

The way the Faalklands work, witches are forbidden to settle with a man and thus becoming pregnant, that's why they have to resort to this. This is an important theme in Maja storyline as she yearns a partner and a child.

She was sent out to survey the Emerald Vale as the High Sister and her divination power found that a danger to the clan was brewing in the vicinities of Shroomburg and needed to be monitored. Thus the merchant role.

She was taught to treat men only as tools but she is quite empathetic due to her powers and struggle to be as disdainful towards them as her sisters.

The actions of the player will decide what Maja will end up doing when she finds out about the Final Éclipse which will influence the ending.
