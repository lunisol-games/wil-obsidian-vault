Anna was the sweetest girl growing up, even nicer and gentler than her sister Belle. On top of that she has always been a very talented and athletic Wayfarer, sometimes qualified as a genius for how much control she has over her teleportation. She was helping around in the village using her powers and was really loved by everyone.

Maybe because of her good nature, or through Selene's influence, Anna is a girl that is very close to the wilderness. She is deeply connected to all creatures that inhabit it.

When she started going pro, she teamed up with Eric, a Heliotrope neighbor, and they gathered and sold magical ingredients in the forest. She really liked the guy and loved the routine of gathering stuff in the forest even more.
However, Eric was greedy, and when they found a Owlfox sanctuary, a rare, precious, and endangered magic creature, Anna made him promise to not tell anyone... Only for him to tip the Merchant Guild of Rospiel for a fat sum of money and getting them all captured or butchered

Since then, Anna became cold and angry towards men and decided to work alone.
