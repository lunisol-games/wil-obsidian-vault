The God of Light and Love. Also tied to Death as he handles the reincarnation cycle of souls.

Lives on a small sun, and has a little paradisiac afterlife kingdom where Witches and Heliotrope go after death unless they prefer to reincarnate.

Super himbo personality, always chasing after Selene's skirt. Although he always appears very boorish and careless, he is nonetheless quite smart and patient, and knows he has all the time in the world to exact his plan of [[07 - The Final Eclipse]].
