The Goddess of Night and Magic. Also tied to Birth as she is the maker of all living things, weaved from the [[Magima]] of [[The Moontree]].

Lives on Earth, inside [[The Moontree]], next to [[The Tyranne Sisterhood]], although she never directly engages with them as she prefer not to interfere with her creations' life.

She is undeniably the most beautiful creature that exists, and women, created to her image, are not even close contenders.

When we finally encounter her, she appears as very dignified, noble, wholesome. However, she is also a huge nonredeemable pervert, quite a lot more stupidly horny than her soulmate. She has very little self control and easily give in to her desires. Like way too easily. It's almost a gag at the end of the game when you're led to believe distracting her is going to be a super hard Challenge but she literally folds in 2 seconds when you propose to fuck.
