Anna was the sweetest girl growing up, even nicer and gentler than her sister Belle. On top of that she has always been a very talented and athletic Wayfarer, sometimes qualified as a genius for how much control she has over her teleportation. She was helping around in the village using her powers and was really loved by everyone.

When she started going pro, she contracted for [[The Guild of Merchants]], and was under the management of a Heliotrope neighbor, Eric, that sometimes accompanied her in her foraging routine.

She really liked that funny unc's company, and loved gathering stuff in the forest even more.
Maybe because of her good nature, or through Selene's influence, Anna is a girl that is very close to the wilderness. She is deeply connected to all creatures that inhabit it. So she was thrilled that Eric was always super interested in the ecosystem in [[Aerafal]], he was even particularly... inquisitive?
In fact, he has secretly taken advantage of her to locate endangered magic species and invaluable rare flowers and trees to tip the Guild so they can get plundered.
Anna only realized after years of working for them and she completely flipped realizing all the damage that's been done to the forest was partly because of her.

Since then, Anna became cold and angry towards men and decided to never ever trust others with secrets of the nature anymore. She also has a visceral hate towards [[The Guild of Merchants]]. And they happen to have very recently set their eyes on the Shroomburg forest...

Depending on how the story goes, the Guild's effort may be thwarted and Anna's trauma may heal.
