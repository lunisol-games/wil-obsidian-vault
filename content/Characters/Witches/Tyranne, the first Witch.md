The most well known witch that was said to have reigned over human society for centuries. She is not the first to benefit from Selene's blessing, but is among the first generations. She has that title because she started to use the term "Witch" to describe her and her sisters, and it became commonly used later.

Her life work is the Witch Sanctuary created quite close to the Moontree, where all Witches are welcome. But no one else.

She also famously "fell" kingdoms and churches, and purged the world of Helios sanctuaries when Heliotropes appeared and weakened the Witches by depleting the Magima in the world.

She had the Witch Factor, although it was not public knowledge. It made her powers scale with her depravity. She secretly was the sluttiest Witch on earth and it's a solid option for a future female mc corruption game for the franchise.

Albeit she is now remembered as a scary, tyrannic, powerful witch that was without pity for normal humans, the truth about her circumstances are quite different and she was sort of led into being involved with influencial people a bit against her own will.

Upon her passing she became Helios' main concubine and serves him in bed diligently and happily ever since. This is her "canon" ending for the games of the franchise.
